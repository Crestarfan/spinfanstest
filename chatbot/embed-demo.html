🌀 SPIN Fans Chatbot Widget – Embed Demo

This page demonstrates the floating chatbot widget embedded in any webpage. The widget button appears in the bottom-right corner. Click it to open the chat.

How to embed on your website

Add the following snippet before the closing </body> tag:

<!-- SPIN Fans Chatbot Widget -->
<script src="knowledge-base.js"></script>
<script src="chatbot-engine.js"></script>
<script src="widget.js"></script>

The widget will auto-initialise and appear as a floating button in the bottom-right corner of your page.
